EulerPilot v2 — BACKGROUND pid target -> class_map — SP3 evidence
Recorded 2026-10-21 on schedpilot-sp3 (<SP3-IP>)

Behavior
  A policy target with kind=pid and role=BACKGROUND is written to the pinned
  class_map after attach:
    key=tgid, klass=BG, confidence=1000, source=SP_SRC_EXTERN,
    updated_ns=CLOCK_MONOTONIC.
  The executor monitor refreshes these entries every 2 s; BPF class entries
  expire after 5 s, so the refresh keeps an active action classified.
  The map lifetime is the attachment: rollback/detach removes it, so there is
  no classification left behind.

Boundaries
  - only explicit pid+BACKGROUND targets are mapped;
  - cgroup targets are not expanded to class_map keys yet;
  - FOREGROUND/NEUTRAL are not forced to LAT/COMP/CACHE, because business
    role is not a runtime characteristic;
  - no automatic demotion of unknown high-CPU tasks.
