EulerPilot v2 — BACKGROUND cgroup target -> class_map — SP3 evidence
Recorded 2026-10-21 on schedpilot-sp3 (<SP3-IP>)

Behavior
  A policy target with kind=cgroup and role=BACKGROUND is expanded to its
  current cgroup.procs members after attach:
    each pid -> class_map[tgid]={klass=BG, confidence=1000, source=EXTERN}
  The executor monitor rescans cgroup.procs every 2 s, so processes that join
  the cgroup while the action is active are classified within the refresh
  window. BPF class entries expire after 5 s, so the 2 s refresh keeps them
  alive. The map lifetime is the attachment; rollback/detach removes it.
  A scan is capped at 256 pids per cgroup.

Boundaries
  - only explicit role=BACKGROUND targets are mapped (pid and cgroup);
  - FOREGROUND/NEUTRAL are not forced to LAT/COMP/CACHE: business role is not
    a runtime characteristic;
  - no automatic demotion of unknown high-CPU tasks;
  - cgroup removal/rename while active is tolerated (scan just stops).

Test scenario
  /sys/fs/cgroup/ep-v2-test-<shell-pid> is created, a `sleep 300` process is
  moved into it, and the policy adds that cgroup with role=BACKGROUND.
  The test asserts class-get(pid) -> class=BG, source=2 (EXTERN), then removes
  the cgroup in cleanup.
