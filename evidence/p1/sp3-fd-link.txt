EulerPilot v2 — FD-passing link ownership — SP3 evidence
Recorded 2026-10-21 on schedpilot-sp3 (<SP3-IP>)

Design
  ep-sched-loader attaches the scheduler once, pins maps, then sends the
  struct_ops bpf_link FD to the executor over a Unix socket (SCM_RIGHTS) and
  exits. The executor holds the link FD; when it crashes, the FD closes and
  the kernel detaches the scheduler. No long-lived loader child, no PDEATHSIG
  dependency for the --skill sched path.
  sched-ref keeps the old child-process mode for comparison/tests.
