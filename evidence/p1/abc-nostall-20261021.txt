What this is
  A liveness check, not a performance measurement. A CPU-bound bash loop runs
  for 2 s under fair, basic and class scheduling; the test asserts that utime
  advances in every arm and that sched_ext state transitions are clean.
  It does NOT compare throughput/latency and must not be cited as a v2 gain.
