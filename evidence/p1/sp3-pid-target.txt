Schema / validator
  spec.targets item now accepts:
    {"kind":"cgroup","path":"/sys/fs/cgroup/...","role":...,"role_source":"explicit"}
    {"kind":"pid","pid":N,"role":...,"role_source":"explicit"}
  cgroup requires path and rejects pid; pid requires 1..4194304 and rejects path.
  Duplicate cgroup paths / duplicate pids are rejected. Unknown kinds are rejected.

Identity
  resolve_targets() for a pid target:
    - pidfd_open(pid), close after the check;
    - read /proc/<pid>/stat field 22 (starttime);
    - record pid + starttime in TargetBinding.
  revalidate_targets() opens the pidfd again and compares starttime; a changed
  starttime (PID reuse) or a dead process -> TARGET_STALE.
  The pidfd is used for validation; it is not held across the transaction yet.
