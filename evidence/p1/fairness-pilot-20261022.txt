Implemented first version
  - struct sp_cfg v3 adds lat_budget_pct (60%), budget_window_ms (100ms),
    aging_ms (20ms); loader defaults and ep-schedctl get/set/restore updated.
  - One effective_policy() is used by select_cpu/enqueue/running/stopping/
    dispatch; ADAPTIVE with stale heartbeat falls back to complete static
    defaults for flags, slices, preempt/migration and budget.
  - Per-CPU fair_budget window accounts runtime in stopping(), so direct
    SCX_DSQ_LOCAL dispatches are charged as well.
  - dispatch: under budget LAT runs first; over budget or when non-LAT aged,
    CACHE/COMP (vtime, includes NORMAL/BG) run first; if no other runnable,
    LAT may borrow the idle budget. COMP/CACHE alternate; BG keeps its vtime
    penalty and made progress.
  - A repeatable probe is in tests/fairness_sp3.sh + tests/fairness_probe.c.

Correction (2026-10-22, later run; numbers above left byte-for-byte intact)
  - CLASSMAP_HIT 14 / CLASSMAP_MISS 1080 were read WITHOUT summing the
    BPF_MAP_TYPE_PERCPU_ARRAY across CPUs. Re-aggregating the same raw dump
    gives hit 2318 (all on CPU7), miss 1848 (750 on CPU7), invalid 0,
    expired 0. The class_map hypothesis below is therefore void.
  - throttle=0 was self-consistent, not a dead counter: at 31% LAT never
    reaches the 60% cap. The share is set by the slice-length ratio because
    DSQ_LAT empties as soon as LAT is moved out; lat_budget_pct only caps.
  - Fixed by per-CPU summation in the pilot, a slice quota
    (lat_slice = comp_slice * pct / (100 - pct)) and wall-time accounting in
    stopping(). See evidence/p1/fairness-budget-20261022.txt.
