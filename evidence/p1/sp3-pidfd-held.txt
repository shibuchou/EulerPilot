Change
  TargetBinding now owns a std::shared_ptr<int> pidfd for pid targets.
  resolve_targets() opens pidfd_open(pid) and keeps it; copies share the fd
  and the last copy closes it. revalidate_targets() uses
  pidfd_send_signal(fd, 0) plus the /proc/<pid>/stat starttime, so a dead or
  recycled PID fails closed even if the PID number is reused.
  cgroup targets are unchanged (inode/device/boot_id/cgroup_id).

  - resolve(getpid()) -> pidfd is non-null and >= 0
  - revalidate passes while the process lives
  - fork a child, resolve it, kill+reap it, revalidate now fails
